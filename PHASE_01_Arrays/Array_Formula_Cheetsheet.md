
# PART 15 — ARRAY REVISION FORMULA SHEET

## Important Mathematical Formulas

**1. Last Index**

$$
LastIndex=n-1
$$

**2. Average**

$$
Average=\frac{Sum}{n}
$$

**3. Sum of First n Natural Numbers**

$$
S=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

**4. Prefix Sum**

$$
prefix[i]=prefix[i-1]+arr[i]
$$

**5. Range Sum**

$$
Sum(L,R)=prefix[R]-prefix[L-1]
$$

**6. Kadane's Algorithm**

$$
current=\max(arr[i],current+arr[i])
$$

$$
maximum=\max(maximum,current)
$$

**7. Missing Number**

$$
Missing=ExpectedSum-ActualSum
$$

**8. Number of Subarrays**

$$
Subarrays=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

**9. Number of Non-Empty Subsequences**

$$
Subsequences=2^n-1
$$

**10. Binary Search Middle**

$$
mid=low+\frac{high-low}{2}
$$

**11. Two Sum**

$$
Required=Target-CurrentElement
$$

**12. Stock Profit**

$$
Profit=SellingPrice-BuyingPrice
$$
